About Me

I'm a UX researcher and filmmaker based in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. I believe the best insights come from genuine human connection and careful observation.

My work sits at the intersection of qualitative research and visual storytelling. As a UX researcher, I uncover the motivations and pain points that drive user behavior. As a filmmaker, I capture the emotional truths that data alone cannot express.

I've worked with startups and established companies across Southeast Asia, helping them build products that truly resonate with their users. My research approach is deeply collaborative—I believe the best solutions emerge when teams work together to understand their users' needs.
